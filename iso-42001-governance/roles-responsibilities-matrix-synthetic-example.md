# AI Management System Roles and Responsibilities — Synthetic Example

> **Fictional teaching sample.** All roles, assignments, and activities below
> are illustrative. This is not a real organisation's responsibility matrix or
> evidence of compliance. Confirm authority and independence before adapting.

- **Organisation:** Example Organisation
- **Document owner:** AI Governance Lead
- **Review:** At least annually and after an organisational or system change

## 1. How to use this matrix

Assign named people to roles through the organisation's controlled appointment
process; do not put personal contact details in a public sample. Confirm that
each role has the authority, competence, time, and access needed to perform its
responsibilities. Where one person holds multiple roles, assess conflicts and
provide an independent review when needed.

## 2. Role definitions

| Role | Core responsibility | Example authority |
|---|---|---|
| Executive sponsor | Ensures governance has direction and appropriate resources. | Escalated risk and resource decisions within delegated authority. |
| Business owner | Defines intended use and accepts business accountability for operating the system. | Proposes use, pauses service, and requests a risk decision. |
| System owner | Maintains system design, controls, monitoring, and operational records. | Implements approved changes and assigns technical actions. |
| Model owner | Maintains model purpose, version, limitations, and performance evidence. | Recommends model changes or withdrawal. |
| AI risk owner | Challenges risk ratings and tracks treatment and acceptance decisions. | Escalates risks under approved criteria. |
| Privacy and security advisers | Review data protection and security impacts within their remit. | Recommend conditions, mitigations, or escalation. |
| Human reviewer | Reviews assigned cases and records a reasoned disposition. | Approves, rejects, or escalates only within assigned authority. |
| Independent assurer | Tests or reviews controls independently of the work being assessed. | Reports findings and verifies evidence against closure criteria. |

## 3. Responsibility assignment

Key: **A** = accountable for the activity; **R** = performs the work;
**C** = consulted; **I** = informed. Each activity should have one accountable
role. Adapt assignments to the organisation's actual structure.

| Activity | Executive sponsor | Business owner | System / model owner | AI risk owner | Privacy / security advisers | Human reviewer | Independent assurer |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Approve intended use and affected-user scope | I | A/R | C | C | C | I | I |
| Prepare system and data-flow description | I | C | A/R | C | C | I | I |
| Assess and record AI risks | I | C | R | A | C | C | I |
| Validate model and workflow behavior | I | C | A/R | C | C | C | C |
| Decide whether release conditions are met | I | A | R | C | C | I | C |
| Review an individual case | I | A | C | I | I | R | I |
| Monitor performance and report incidents | I | C | A/R | C | C | C | I |
| Approve risk acceptance within authority | C | C | I | A/R | C | I | I |
| Independently verify a corrective action | I | I | C | C | C | I | A/R |
| Review system retirement and record retention | I | A | R | C | C | I | C |

Do not treat consultation as approval. A reviewer who performs the control
should not be the sole independent assurer of that control.

## 4. Evidence of assignment and action

For each real appointment or decision, keep the approved record in the
organisation's access-controlled system. A record may include:

- Role identifier and scope of authority.
- Appointment or delegation approval and effective period.
- Required competence or training.
- Decision reference, rationale, and supporting evidence.
- Any conflict-of-interest assessment and independent review.
- Review trigger, expiry, or replacement when responsibilities change.

Use role identifiers in broadly shared documents. Store individual names and
contact information only in an approved restricted directory when required.

## 5. Review checklist

Before approving this matrix, verify that:

1. Every governance activity has one accountable role.
2. Decision authority and escalation paths are explicit.
3. Preparation, approval, and independent assurance are appropriately
   separated.
4. Deputies and temporary delegations have defined limits.
5. Changes in people, structure, systems, or obligations trigger review.
