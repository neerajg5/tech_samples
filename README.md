# Synthetic AI Governance and Security Samples

This repository contains fictional teaching examples for AI governance,
security risk analysis, and threat modeling. The documents are starting points
for discussion, not records of real systems, risk assessments, controls, or
compliance.

## Repository structure

### `agentic_data_engg/`

Examples for threat modeling and security analysis of agentic and AI-enabled
systems:

- [AI risk register](https://github.com/neerajg5/tech_samples/blob/main/agentic_data_engg/ai-risk-register-synthetic-example.md) —
  records illustrative AI risks, ownership, treatment, and review triggers.
- [AI technique coverage matrix](https://github.com/neerajg5/tech_samples/blob/main/agentic_data_engg/atlas-ttp-coverage-synthetic-example.md) —
  maps example AI-specific techniques to controls and evidence.
- [Endpoint register](https://github.com/neerajg5/tech_samples/blob/main/agentic_data_engg/endpoint-register-synthetic-example.yaml) —
  demonstrates a YAML inventory for documenting HTTP endpoints and security
  review details.
- [PASTA threat analysis](https://github.com/neerajg5/tech_samples/blob/main/agentic_data_engg/pasta-scenarios-synthetic-example.md) —
  provides fictional threat scenarios and example risk scoring.
- [STRIDE threat register](https://github.com/neerajg5/tech_samples/blob/main/agentic_data_engg/stride-register-synthetic-example.md) —
  illustrates tracking threats, controls, evidence, and residual risk.

### `iso-42001-governance/`

Fictional governance documents illustrating policy, management-system
structure, and accountability:

- [AI governance policy](https://github.com/neerajg5/tech_samples/blob/main/iso-42001-governance/ai-governance-policy-synthetic-example.md) —
  outlines example principles, scope, and policy-level responsibilities.
- [AI management system charter](https://github.com/neerajg5/tech_samples/blob/main/iso-42001-governance/aims-charter-synthetic-example.md) —
  describes example boundaries, governance processes, objectives, and review.
- [Roles and responsibilities matrix](https://github.com/neerajg5/tech_samples/blob/main/iso-42001-governance/roles-responsibilities-matrix-synthetic-example.md) —
  shows illustrative accountability across preparation, approval, operation,
  and assurance.

## Important limitations

All organisations, systems, roles, scenarios, measures, and workflows in these
samples are invented. The examples do not describe a real organisation or
establish compliance with a standard or law. They contain no real customer,
regulator, employee, or operational data.

Before adapting a sample, confirm its scope, terminology, legal and regulatory
context, approval authority, and evidence requirements with relevant business,
risk, compliance, privacy, security, and domain owners. Replace illustrative
statements with approved policy and verified controls. Do not use these
examples as evidence of implementation or certification.
